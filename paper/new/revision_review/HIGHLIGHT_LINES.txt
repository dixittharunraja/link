Device Meshes via Transitive Trust-on-First-Use
(T-TOFU)
Abstract—Modern multi-device environments, encompassing
smartphones, personal computers and embedded endpoints,
frequently demand tight local network interoperability for
telemetry, continuous synchronization and remote peripheral
control.
Despite the operational demand for continuous local inter-
operability, establishing secure and user-friendly trust across
these endpoints remains fundamentally challenging. System
architectures in this domain inevitably encounter the Usable-
