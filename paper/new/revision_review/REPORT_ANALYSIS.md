## Formulaic prose versus necessary terminology

The generic opening about “Modern multi-device environments”, the introduction’s “contemporary computing landscape”, and the unsupported universal trilemma framing obscure the actual contribution. These are editorial judgments about specificity and evidence; they are not explanations of why Turnitin highlighted a sentence. The first introduction paragraph was not highlighted.

They are not replaced with synonyms. Clear highlighted wording—such as the 128-bit identifier specification and the revocation flooding step—is retained. All ten displayed equations are unchanged.
