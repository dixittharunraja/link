# Code of Conduct

We want Link's issues, pull requests and discussions to be a place where people can work together without being
treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful, and take feedback the same way.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here was new once.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about someone's background, identity or ability.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly pushing for something after a decision has been made.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a
problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact a maintainer through the repository's Security tab
if it is sensitive. Reports are handled privately.
