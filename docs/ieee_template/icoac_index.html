ICoAC 2026

ICoAC 2026 is the 13th International Conference on Advanced Computing organized by the Department of Computer Technology, MIT Campus, Anna University, Chennai.. Centered around the theme “Actionable AI for Sustainability and Resilience”, the conference serves as a premier global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to exchange innovative ideas and present cutting-edge research in advanced computing technologies. ICoAC 2026 will feature keynote lectures, technical sessions, tutorials, and research presentations spanning domains such as Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Quantum Computing, Sustainable Computing, Cloud and Edge Computing, IoT, Healthcare AI, Cyber-Physical Systems, Autonomous Systems, and Intelligent Networks.
